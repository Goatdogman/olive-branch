— The Ask

Come to Rhode Island. Before the wedding.

It has been a long time. There is a day in August that everyone is going to be at, and that day should belong to my sister and not to whatever the rest of us are still carrying.

So I am offering this one first.

— The Plan

Drive over. Shoot at the range. Come back here for a beer and dinner.

The Range is a clean, regulated, professional place. I am a member. They walk through the safety briefing whether anyone has shot before or not. No one needs to bring anything. I will cover the lane and the ammo. After, we drive back to the house and eat.

— The Honest Part

I am not pretending nothing happened.

I am saying that August is not the place for it. A wedding is not the place for it. And if we do not put something on the calendar before then, it will find its way into that day on its own. I do not want that for my sister.

So pick a Saturday. Tell me which one works. I will be here.

It does not have to be like this.
